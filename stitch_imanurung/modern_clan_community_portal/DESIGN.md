## Brand & Style

This design system serves a modern clan governance and community management platform. The design aesthetic balances dignified enterprise SaaS rigor with cultural resonance rooted in North Sumatran Batak heritage (Punguan Marga). 

The visual style blends modern corporate clarity with tactile, premium touches:
- **Dignity & Heritage:** Grounded navy foundations communicate institutional trust, inter-generational continuity, and administrative authority.
- **Vitality & Kinship:** Expressive crimson accents evoke the traditional *ulos* textile palette, lending warmth, celebration, and active engagement to an otherwise disciplined interface.
- **Prestige & Integrity:** Brass gold accents distinguish verified lineage, honorary statuses, and formal governance actions.
- **Enterprise Execution:** Crisp light slate surfaces, measured whitespace, subtle container depths, and high-legibility typography provide a frictionless operational environment for registry audits, dues collection, event registrations, and genealogical mapping (Tarombo).

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, high-contrast hierarchy suitable for civic accountability, member registries, and community interaction.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#0E243A` - Heritage Midnight Navy):** The structural core. Used for sidebar navigation, primary structural buttons, active tabs, major headings, and focal iconography. Conveys institutional permanence and administrative gravitas.
- **Secondary (`#B91C1C` - Ulos Crimson):** The cultural beacon and primary call-to-action anchor. Applied to vital state changes, active notifications, prominent member actions, event badges, and cultural milestone markers.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Brass Gold):** Reserved for prestige states, verified ancestral lineage badges (*Tarombo Sah*), traditional committee designations, and financial reconciliation alerts.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8FAFC` - Clean Slate):** Base application canvas. Paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces to build readable content layers.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Muted structural dividers providing micro-definition without visual noise.
- **Text Hierarchies:** Deep charcoal (`#0F172A`) for primary body and headers; slate neutral (`#334155`) for secondary metadata and table descriptions; muted slate (`#64748B`) for helper text and inactive states.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric construction conveys modern enterprise authority, while its friendly terminals bring warmth to community-focused touchpoints.

### Typography Guidelines
- **Display & Headings:** Use tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and solid semi-bold or bold weights. These preserve clarity in multi-line clan declarations, sector titles, and statistical highlights.
- **Lineage & Data Presentation:** Tables, genealogical trees, and financial statements default to `body-md` and `label-md` to maximize density without sacrificing readability.
- **Numbers and Metrics:** Dashboard cards and financial tallies use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain alignment across columns.
- **Cultural Subheadings:** Formal Indonesian / Batak titles (e.g., *Pengurus Pusat*, *Pomparan*) use `label-sm` in uppercase with wider tracking (`0.04em`) to establish visual order.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptive, 8-point structural system, maintaining clarity across dense data views and celebratory community feeds.

### Layout Model
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed-collapsible navigation sidebar (260px expanded, 72px icon-only) with a fluid content container constrained to a maximum width of 1440px. The main workspace employs a 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` outer page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Navigation collapses to an off-canvas drawer or streamlined bottom action bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` column gutters. Multi-column cards and lineage trees stack into linear scroll blocks or accordion modules.

### Vertical Spacing Cadence
- Content sections are spaced using `space-xl` (`2.5rem`).
- Internal card grouping uses `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- Micro element groups (e.g., input icons, labels, badge arrays) use `space-xs` (`0.25rem`) and `space-sm` (`0.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean tonal surfaces, subtle dark navy-tinted ambient shadows, and selective 1px border containment.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` provides the foundational canvas.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Used for data cards, tables, and standard lists.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals & Flyouts):** Pure `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(14, 36, 58, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(14, 36, 58, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (System Overlays & Tooltips):** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0E243A`) surfaces with crisp white micro-copy for informative popovers and dropdown states.

### Cultural Border Motifs
Key summary cards and formal certificate views incorporate an optional top-edge accent: a 3px structured border in Ulos Crimson (`#B91C1C`) or Warm Brass Gold (`#D97706`), honoring traditional textile borders without cluttering the screen.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced geometric curve language to deliver a contemporary enterprise software experience.

### Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Checkboxes):** `0.5rem` (8px) provides functional containment and fits dense form layouts.
- **Cards & Data Modules (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) offers gentle framing for charts, member profiles, and dashboard statistics.
- **Hero Banners, Modals & Shell Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) softens large structural blocks.
- **Pill Controls:** Full circular pills (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for status badges, tags, and avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0E243A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, corner radius `0.5rem`. Hover state transitions to `#1A365D`. Focus state features a 2px offset ring of `#0E243A`.
- **Secondary (Accent Pop):** Background `#B91C1C`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state transitions to `#991B1B`. Used for high-priority initiatives, event RSVPs, or member actions.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Background transparent, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9` with a border color of `#CBD5E1`.
- **Pill CTA:** Padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, font `label-lg`, with smooth transitions (`150ms ease-in-out`).

### Badges & Chips
- **Lineage / Verification (Brass Gold):** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`. Used for verified branches and elders.
- **Clan Dues / Status Active (Emerald):** Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#166534`, border 1px solid `#BBF7D0`.
- **Administrative / Council (Navy):** Background `#E0E7FF`, text `#1E1B4B`, border 1px solid `#C7D2FE`.
- **Shape & Spacing:** Pill-shaped radius (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, typography `label-sm`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Input Elements:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Text `body-md` in `#0F172A`.
- **States:** Hover border shifts to `#94A3B8`. Focus border shifts to `#0E243A` accompanied by an ambient box-shadow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 36, 58, 0.1)`). Invalid states switch to `#B91C1C` with an error text label below.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, radius `1rem` (16px), padding `1.5rem`.
- **Interactive Card:** Includes hover transition: elevation shadow shifts to `0 12px 20px -4px rgba(14, 36, 58, 0.06)`, subtle translateY (-2px).
- **Heritage Header Card:** White background with a distinct 4px top accent line in Crimson (`#B91C1C`) or Brass Gold (`#D97706`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Base Checkbox:** Dimensions `18px x 18px`, radius `0.25rem`, border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#0E243A` displaying a white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular `18px x 18px`, active state features a `#0E243A` ring with a `#0E243A` center dot.

### Specialized Clan Components
- **Tarombo Tree Nodes (Genealogy Graph):** Compact white rounded containers (`rounded-lg`) with 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`, featuring generation level tags (`label-sm`), member full name (`title-md`), and status indicators. Lineage connector strokes use `#94A3B8` at 2px thickness.
- **Iuran / Dues Metric Progress Bar:** Background track `#E2E8F0` with height `8px`, radius `9999px`. The progress fill defaults to Heritage Navy (`#0E243A`) transitioning to Ulos Crimson (`#B91C1C`) as target deadlines approach.
